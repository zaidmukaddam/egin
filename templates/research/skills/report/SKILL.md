---
description: Structure for a written research report
---
Write the report as Markdown:

1. `# <Question>` then a two-sentence answer.
2. `## Findings`: one bullet per finding, each ending with its source URL.
3. `## Open questions`: what the sources did not settle.
4. `## Sources`: every URL used, one per line, with the date it was published when known.

Keep it under 600 words. Do not pad with background the reader did not ask for.
