---
description: Copy for a landing page, section by section
---
Write the page as Markdown, one `##` per section, with the button text in brackets: `[Start free]`.

1. Hero: the one claim from `brand.md` as the headline, under ten words. A subhead that says what it is and who it's for. One primary button, named for the action.
2. Proof right under the hero: the strongest number or logo you have, with its source.
3. Three to four sections, one job each: the problem in the buyer's words, how it works, what it replaces, what it costs. Each with a heading that says something on its own; a reader skimming only headings should get the pitch.
4. Objections: the two or three reasons a buyer hesitates, answered plainly.
5. The same button again, with one line under it that removes the last doubt (no card needed, free tier, open source).

Every heading and button names something specific. "Powerful features" and "Learn more" say nothing.
