---
description: Structure for a launch or release post
---
1. `# <What shipped, in plain words>`. Not a tagline: "Reports can run on a schedule" beats "A new era of reporting".
2. Two sentences: what you can do now that you couldn't yesterday, and who it's for.
3. Show it. A command to run, a short example, or what the screen shows, before any explanation.
4. How it works, in three to five short paragraphs or a short list. One idea each.
5. The proof from `brand.md` that this release adds to: a number, a benchmark, a quote you were given.
6. How to get it: install or upgrade steps, the version, a link to the docs.
7. One line on what's next, only if it's decided.

Under 600 words unless the brief says otherwise. No "we're excited", no closing summary of what you already said.
