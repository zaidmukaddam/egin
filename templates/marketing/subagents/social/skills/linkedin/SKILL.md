---
description: Writing posts for LinkedIn
---
- The first two lines show before "see more". Put the result or the claim there, not a scene-setting opener.
- 80 to 200 words. Short paragraphs of one or two sentences, with a blank line between them.
- Write as a person who built or used the thing, in the first person. Company voice reads as an ad.
- One concrete detail per paragraph: a number, a name, what happened.
- End with a question people can answer from their own work, or with the link. Not both, and not "Thoughts?".
- No hashtag block, no emoji bullets, no "I'm humbled to announce".
