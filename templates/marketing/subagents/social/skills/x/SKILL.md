description: Writing posts for X, built on what its published ranking code rewards
---
X publishes the code that ranks the For You feed (github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm). It scores a post by how likely each viewer is to take each action, and the actions carry very different weights. Write for the heavy ones.

What the code rewards:
- Replies, above all from people who follow each other, are the strongest positive signal. A line people want to answer beats a line people want to like.
- Quotes, reposts and shares in DMs come next. Likes count for little.
- Negative feedback (mute, block, "not interested", report) outweighs every positive signal. A jab at a named person costs more reach than it earns.
- Quote a post rather than repost it: X hides a repost from people who already saw the original, but keeps the quote.
- The same author's third post in someone's feed that day is worth much less than the first. One or two posts a day.
- There's no term for links, hashtags or time of day in the scoring. Don't move a link into a reply "for the algorithm".

How to write it:
- Under 15 words when you can; 280 characters is the limit, not the target.
- One idea per post. Lead with the result or the surprising fact, not the setup.
- Specific beats grand: "runs 3x faster on the same model" over "blazing fast".
- A screenshot, chart or short video carries the numbers. The text is the reaction to it.
- A thread only when each post holds up alone. Otherwise, one post and a link.
- No hashtags, no 🚀 or 🧵, no "here's why", no "let that sink in", no "we're excited to announce".
